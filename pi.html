Dr. Xiangang Huang, Principal Investigator

Dr. Xiangang Huang is an Associate Professor at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, School of Chemistry and Chemical Engineering. His research focuses on developing oral devices and platforms to deliver nucleic acids, such as mRNA and siRNA.

E-mail: xghuang@sjtu.edu.cn
